Delete your CareNest account

You can delete your account inside the CareNest app:

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter the code we text to your phone to confirm.

Your name, phone number, email, family members and saved addresses are erased. Booking and payment records are kept for accounting, with your personal details removed.

If you have an upcoming booking, please call support first: {{ support_phone }}.

Cannot open the app? Call support and we will delete the account for you.
